Who will hold my account?

Absel Global is a trading name of Absel Markets, licensed by the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius under licence no. GB25205423. Your account and your money are held by the licensed company.

Is my money safe?

Client funds are held in segregated bank accounts, separate from the company's own operational funds, as the FSC requires. Professional indemnity insurance adds a further layer of protection. Trading itself carries risk and you can lose money.

STP or ECN: which should I choose?

STP has no commission, with spreads from 0.9 pips. ECN offers raw spreads from 0.2 pips plus a commission from $7 per lot. Scalpers, EA users and high-volume traders usually prefer ECN.

Which platform will I trade on?

MetaTrader 5 on Windows, in your web browser, and on iOS and Android, with optional VPS hosting for Expert Advisors.

Do you offer swap-free accounts?

Yes. Islamic accounts are Sharia-compliant with no overnight swap charges.

How do I deposit?

Visa, Mastercard, bank wire, USDT, Skrill, Neteller and UnionPay. Absel Global charges 0% on deposits.

Which countries can't open an account?

Absel Global does not accept residents of the United Arab Emirates, United States, Canada, Singapore, Cuba, Eritrea, Myanmar, Iran, North Korea, Kyrgyzstan, Syria, Liberia, Yemen, Somalia, Jamaica, Albania, Barbados, Cambodia or Panama.
